# Command Center walkthrough (v0.3.0)

The Command Center is the app's home page. It tells **one story in seven chapters**, and every figure comes from the certified gold views; no AI calculates any of them. "Today" in the data is **Tue 20 Oct 2026**. The chapters cover **September 2026**, the last full month.

The figures below come from the deployed v0.3.0 data (synthetic, calibrated to Optus's figures). If the data is regenerated, check them again.

## Executive summary (7 lines, each one jumps to its chapter)

1. **September:** 702 new sales from 17,049 calls, 4.1% against 5.4%. That is **219 sales short**, about $190,413 of first-year revenue.
2. **Where sales are lost:** mobile is the biggest call reason (2,365 calls) but converts only **3.8%**, against 15.7% for new NBN. 683 customers who wanted to buy didn't.
3. **Jarvis:** **115** sales stopped, costing 0.7 points of conversion. Tickets take 3.8 days on average.
4. **Cross-sell:** **302 of 507** home internet sales had no mobile offered. When it is offered, 33% say yes.
5. **The first call:** only **24 of 683** lost sales were won back. In 518 of 1,348 promised callbacks, the customer ended up calling us.
6. **Coaching:** the 10 consultants furthest below the median convert 2.1% to 5.4% of their calls, against a 6.8% median. That is **205 sales** below the median over 3 months.
7. **Closing the gap:** fix Jarvis (86), offer the cross-sell (137) and coach discovery (61). Together that is about **284 sales a month** against a gap of 219.

**Bottom line shown on the page:** fixing those three things is *enough to close* the gap.

## Chapter 5: What happens when the first call doesn't close?

**Lost on the first call, lost for good:**
- 683 lost sales.
- 384 of those customers (56%) called again within 14 days.
- Only **24 (4%)** bought on that call.
- A first call converts **8.9%**; a repeat call converts **3.4%**.

**Promised callbacks and repeat calls:**
- 1,348 callbacks were promised.
- In **518 (38%)** of them, the customer had to call us instead.
- There were 6,369 repeat calls, and **4,328 (68%)** came within the hour, which means something broke on the first call.

**Where transfers go (4,374 transfers; 2,673 from sales calls; 730 avoidable, 17%, worth about 4.5 points of FCR):**

Only the top three queues' shares come from Optus; the rest are synthetic until Optus shares them.
