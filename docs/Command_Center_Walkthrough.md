# Command Center walkthrough (v0.3.0, October view)

The Command Center is the app's home page. It tells **one story in seven chapters**, and every figure comes from the certified gold views; no AI calculates any of them.

"Today" in the data is **Tue 20 Oct 2026**. Every chapter shows **this month so far: 1–20 October 2026**. September 2026, the last full month, appears only in small grey reference lines under the figures.

The figures below come from the deployed v0.3.0 data (synthetic, calibrated to Optus's figures). If the data is regenerated, check them again.

## Questions to expect

- **Why is October's gap (118) smaller than September's (219)?** October is 20 days, not a full month. The gap is measured on the calls so far. The hero shows where the month is heading: 668 against 851 at this pace, with 420 more needed in 11 days.
- **Why does recent repeat-call data look low?** The won-back, callback and FCR figures count a repeat call within 14 days. Calls from the last two weeks of October haven't had their full 14 days yet, so these numbers will rise as the month closes.
- **Why does the Assistant say September when I ask about "last month"?** "Last month" means September. The Command Center shows this month (October). Ask "this month" for October.
- **Why is cross-sell take-up 24% here but 33% in September?** Both measure sold ÷ offered on home internet sales in their own month. October has only 59 offers, so the rate moves a lot. The recovery plan uses 24% across every cross-sell since August.
