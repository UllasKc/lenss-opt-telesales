# LensS Telesales Intelligence for Optus

Decision intelligence for Optus telesales, built on Databricks by the Concentrix Data & Analytics Practice and presented as **Concentrix LensS**. It is the same platform as LensS Optus Collections (`D:\Optus_LensS_Collections`), set up for Optus telesales with synthetic demo data shaped to Optus's own figures. Governed bronze → silver → gold data feeds a web app with four tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Command Center** | Where we are today against target (latest day, month to date, pace), an executive summary ("the month in 30 seconds"), then one story in seven chapters: last month and the weekly trend, where sales are lost, what Jarvis costs, whether we ask for the second sale, what happens when the first call doesn't close, who needs coaching, and how the gap to target closes (recovery plan and the five scenarios). Every figure opens the calls behind it |
| **Explorer** | Self-service drill-down by month, team lead, product, call reason, sales category, tenure, state, customer type, call direction, call result, lost-sale driver and FCR outcome, with a two-dimension heatmap, sales and FCR-outcome charts, lost sales, the monthly trend and the call records behind each item (CSV export) |
| **Assistant** | Plain-language questions answered by a Genie space ("LensS query engine"): Quick answer, Deep analysis or Auto, with a conversation-aware router, conversation memory, charts, SQL and a quality score |
| **Observability** | Pipeline traces, answer quality and faithfulness, performance, drift, security and guardrails, Evaluations, Responsible AI and Databricks cost |

Every Command Center and Explorer figure comes from certified SQL views; no AI computes them. Current version: **v0.3.1** (see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)). Built from LensS Optus Collections (commit e9e3b47).

## The story the Command Center tells

Leaders review the dashboard in the last 10 days of the month, so "today" is **Tuesday 20 October 2026** and the data stops there:

Five chapters, one story: are we on target, where are sales lost, the systems cause, the people cause, and how to close the gap. Every chapter shows October so far (1–20 October), with September in small reference lines. The [walkthrough](docs/Command_Center_Walkthrough.md) has every figure.

1. **Are we on target?** October so far converts 4.2% against the 5.4% target (the Concentrix slide showed 4.0% MTD). On this pace the month closes near 668 new sales against 851 needed; hitting target would take about 7.5% over the last 11 days.
2. **Where sales are lost:** "New Mobile Plan / Port In" is the biggest reason customers call, but only 5.4% of those calls sell, against 14.8% for new NBN. 245 customers who wanted to buy were lost to causes Optus can fix: Systems 54%, People 46%. A **lost sale** counts only People and System causes. Customer reasons (failed credit check, ID, port-in account number, price: 172 in October) are shown beside lost sales but not counted. Optus's 43.3 / 25.8 / 30.9 split includes them; without them it is People 45%, System 55%.
3. **Systems: Jarvis:** Jarvis errors stopped 65 sales (0.6 points, as Optus reported); error calls take twice as long.
4. **People: the call:** 113 sales lost to discovery, ownership and transfers; 172 of 296 home internet sales had no mobile offered (cross-sell is new since August and the team is in training); 295 of 765 promised callbacks were left to the customer; 509 transfers were avoidable.
5. **Closing the gap:** fixing Jarvis, the cross-sell and discovery would have added about 172 new sales so far, more than the 118-sale gap.

Who to coach first (the consultants furthest below the median, over the last three months) is at the bottom of the Explorer tab.

## The data

`Data/Optus_GenBI_Telesales_Demo_Data_v1.xlsx`: **synthetic**, generated by [scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py](scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py) (fixed seed) from what Optus and the team leads shared: `emails.txt`, `TELESALESservices.docx` (plans and October sales), the FCR audit file and the Concentrix FCR and Sales Conversion slides. The September slide weeks follow the slides; the generator hits Optus's full-month October table, then cuts the data at 20 October (CUTOFF), so only October's first 20 days are shown. `Data/review/` holds the smaller review copy sent to leadership, with the open questions.

| Sheet | Lands in | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Telesales_Calls_Fact` | `bronze.fact_calls` → `silver` → gold | 181,454 handled calls, 1 Nov 2025 to 20 Oct 2026: reason, result (Sale / Callback / No Answer / Rejected), sale, credit / ID / port-in, lost-sale driver (Customer / People / System) and reason, Jarvis error and ticket, cross-sell (from August 2026), transfer to Optus's queues, repeat within 14 days, FCR |
| `Sales_Orders` | `bronze.sales_orders` | 13,176 order lines (a cross-sell is its own line), plan and revenue |
| `Product_Catalog` | `bronze.product_catalog` | 18 plans and prices (assumed prices marked) |
| `Agent_Dim` | `bronze.agent_dim` | 8 team leads (synthetic names) and 65 active consultants (real names from the FCR file; their figures are synthetic) and 3 former consultants (synthetic names) |
| `Elevate_Audits`, `FCR_Audits` | `bronze.*` | Quality audits (cross-sell fields of the revised form) and FCR audits in the team leads' layout |
| `Targets`, `Data_Dictionary`, `Scenario_Guide`, `Sample_BI_Questions`, `Assumptions_and_Questions` | `context.*` | Reference and governance |

How silver reads it: Y/N flags become 1/0; a flag that doesn't apply ('N/A') becomes NULL (cross-sell attempted without an opportunity, FCR on an unanswered callback, transfer validity without a transfer).

Definitions: conversion = sales ÷ handled calls (new sales count order lines, cross-sells included); FCR, transfer and repeat rates are over answered calls; calls in the last 14 days are "unlocked". Targets live in `gold.business_rules_config`: only the 5.4% new sales conversion target comes from Concentrix; renewals, FCR, cross-sell and handle-time targets are assumptions to confirm.

Not in the data, and said so wherever it matters: credit scores (Jarvis shows eligibility only), CSAT/NPS, queue wait times, other-channel data, device prices and repayments.

## Deploy everything with one command

```bash
python deploy/deploy.py --config deploy/config/personal.json
```

Same steps as LensS Optus Collections (`schemas`, `ingest`, `context`, `transform`, `views`, `summary`, `genie`, `lakebase`, `app`, `smoke`), idempotent; run part of it with `--only` or `--skip`. Its own names, so it runs beside Optus Collections in the same workspace without touching it:

| | Telesales | (Collections) |
|---|---|---|
| Schemas | `optus_telesales_{bronze,silver,gold,context}` | `optus_collections_*` |
| Genie space | LensS Optus Telesales Analytics | LensS Optus Collections Analytics |
| App | `lenss-optus-telesales` | `lenss-optus-collections` |
| Lakebase | project `lenss-collections-app`, database `chatapp-telesales` | same project, `chatapp-optus` |

The transform step checks the data against the workbook: 181,454 calls, 13,176 order lines, September 2026 sales by category (510 / 290 / 230 / 164 / 28) and the scenario counts (302 / 115 / 1,957 / 730 / 288).

Setup, prerequisites, giving people access, idle stop, cost and troubleshooting are as in [SETUP_GUIDE.md](SETUP_GUIDE.md) (from LensS Collections; its examples name that project).
